Inspiration — steal list, not a mood board

Award sites are mostly marketing microsites. We need a desktop jukebox: glass as a layer, buttons that feel powered-on, Winamp density. Hover the demo buttons at the bottom — that’s “alive.”

What we take: Apple Liquid Glass’s idea that controls are a distinct glass layer sitting above content, with a specular highlight that moves on hover/press. Teenage Engineering’s “the button is a physical object.” Somefolk’s restraint so the transport isn’t theatre.
What we don’t: Awwwards 3D camera-scroll album sites (Bastille / SIRUP / Noomo). Pretty. Wrong product.
Recommended · material

Apple Liquid Glass (WWDC 2025)

iOS 26 / macOS Tahoe. Translucent material that behaves like real glass — speculars react to motion. Controls float above the app, not painted into it.

Steal: chips, transport, LCD as glass pills over the void/artwork. Not a frosted rectangle filling the window.

Recommended · alive buttons

Teenage Engineering OP-1

Not an Awwwards site. Hardware: every key has travel, light, and a click. Software that copies TE never uses flat grey rectangles.

Steal: press scale 0.96, edge specular sweep, playing state = breathing glow. Dead buttons are a bug.

Steal: transport is 4 controls, not 14. Our RE-Tune panel is the extra; the player stays dumb and fast.
