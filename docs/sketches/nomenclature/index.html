ShakraFyr nomenclature

How the window names things. Internal ids stay concert_a / solfeggio_lock. This is the language a person should see.

Two jobs

Concert pitch Move the whole orchestra so A sits at a chosen Hertz. The song keeps its key. Default is Verdi 432. was: Concert A
Tone lock Pin a named tone onto a scale degree. A is solved from that lock — never forced equal to the tone. was: Solfeggio lock
Modulation A rate, not a note. Brain-rhythm and Schumann live here. Play/Save will not invent a fake A.
Bypass Hear the original. No retune. Presets dim.

How a lock becomes a retune

1. Named tone528 Hz, 639 Hz, OM 136…
→
2. Lock noteC5, D♯5, C♯3… the degree that wears that Hertz.
→
3. Concert A falls outThe rest of the 12-tone grid slides with it. Ratio = target A / source A.

Never set A itself to 528. That would transpose the song by about three semitones. The tiles under the story (Source A4 / Target A4 / Lock / Ratio) are the math. The paragraph above them is the lore.

Two hearts — do not mix the maps

Heart C528 · MI Love, repair, coming back to center. Solfeggio syllable MI. Marketing often calls this “the heart frequency.” In the 7-chakra set it is solar plexus. Lock: C = 528. A ≈ 444.
Heart 639 · Anahata The 7-chakra heart. Connection, the space between two people. Lock: D♯ = 639. A ≈ 452. Different job from 528.

Worlds

A = 432Cosmic / Verdi. Owns OM 136 and 1152. C5 here is ~514, not 528.
A ≈ 444Solfeggio world. C5 = 528 produces this A. Most “angel numbers” live here (111 × 4 = 444).
A = 440ISO 16. The grid most records, DAWs, and school bands assume.

A=432 and C=528 cannot both be exact in 12-tone equal temperament (~47 ¢ apart). Two intents → two renders.
